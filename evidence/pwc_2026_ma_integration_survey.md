## What the 46% figure refers to
Per the search-index excerpt of the PwC page (execution systems section): 46% of all respondents said their deal value drivers had been fully turned into executable initiatives before integration began, each with a named owner, a timeline and identified dependencies. The rate was 59% for successful acquirers and 28% for limited-success acquirers. PwC calls this 31-point gap the clearest evidence that value creation depends on the execution system more than on the quality of the synergy case.

Caution: the 46% figure was not found in a separate search pass over non-PwC sites. A non-PwC 46% figure from the KPMG 2026 Deal Market Study (policy impact on M&A intentions) is unrelated and must not be confused with it. A PwC Middle East survey and a PwC CEO snapshot also carry 46% figures unrelated to integration.
