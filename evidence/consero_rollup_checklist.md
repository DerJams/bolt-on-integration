## Summary
A marketing page describing Consero's first-month integration process for add-ons. It presents a large internal checklist (496 items, built over 180+ integrations) as the core of the method. It stresses keeping payroll, critical vendors and invoicing running on day one, and names cash-basis books and inconsistent systems as the recurring problems. All figures are vendor self-reports.

## Extracted claims
- [metric] Checklist of 496 items, refined over more than 180 acquisition integrations (vendor self-report).
- [metric] Claims 100+ acquisition integrations supported in the prior year and 50+ companies prepared for exit.
- [metric] Cites that half of finance leaders plan add-on or bolt-on deals in the next 12 months.
- [timing] Typical add-on integration takes about one month; the FAQ says roughly 30 days for mid-market targets.
- [timing] Larger targets (about $50M to $100M revenue) can take 60 to 90 days.
- [timing] Work begins within about a month of LOI, but full system access usually comes only after close.
- [failure-mode] Acquired small businesses keep cash-basis books on SMB software, so conversion to accrual/GAAP is needed before the numbers can be trusted.
- [failure-mode] Missing history: starting balances, prior trial balances and work papers are hard to get from cash-basis targets.
- [failure-mode] Inconsistent systems across locations make consolidated, comparable reporting unreliable.
- [failure-mode] Thin accounting talent at acquired companies (no senior-level finance staff).
- [failure-mode] A clean-up backlog at the platform company carries into each add-on.
- [gate] Standardize the platform first (chart of accounts, AP workflow, reporting, processes) so each add-on maps onto one template.
- [field] A standard chart of accounts is set on the platform, then replicated and mapped for each add-on.
- [gate] Day-one priorities: payroll continuity, payment of business-critical vendors, uninterrupted invoicing.
- [field] Identify the top 10 to 15 business-critical vendors and pay them first (vendor master triage).
- [timing] Payroll runs on the 15th and 30th and must not slip during the transition.
- [field] Invoicing restarts from the target's customer aging report to find unbilled and unpaid items (customer master and AR source).
- [gate] Pre-close runway mapped against the checklist, with a formal finance function assessment before the tech rollout.
- [gate] Process implementation includes AP rules, approval workflow, approvers and payers.
- [gate] Separate implementation, delivery and clean-up teams run in parallel.
- [field] A predetermined, curated tech stack is rolled onto each add-on. This page names no ERP; Consero case studies elsewhere mention Sage Intacct.
- [failure-mode] A messy month-end close undermines even good deals, so close quality works as a failure signal.
