## Summary
A short insight piece. It argues that platforms that can absorb add-ons repeatably earn higher valuations, and it lists five keys: integration strategy/playbook, revenue retention, technology and data, cost synergies, and an accelerated timeline. It is high level and does not address master data or chart of accounts directly.
