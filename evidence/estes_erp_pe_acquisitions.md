## Summary
The article frames the post-acquisition ERP choice as an operating decision with two paths: move the add-on onto the parent ERP, or keep it separate and integrate the data and processes that must connect. It gives eight decision factors and a specific list of master data defects, and it warns that a temporary multi-ERP state can harden into permanent fragmentation. It contains no statistics.
