# Defect taxonomy

Injection decisions (the "must be injected?" column) are recorded in `docs/injection_decisions.md`: made by an independent reviewing model and approved by the repo owner.

Status: template. Rows exist only where an `evidence/` extract describes the failure mode. The column "present naturally in real data?" is taken from `entities/profile.md` (customer and vendor rows) and from a scan of the four charts in `coa/` (chart-of-accounts rows). The column "must be injected?" is left `[FILL]` for the repo owner. Example wording is `[FILL]` where neither the real data nor the evidence settles it; examples given are real records or are quoted from evidence, never invented.

Outcome labels (only these may be used): AUTO_RESOLVE_MATCH, AUTO_RESOLVE_NON_MATCH, ESCALATE_AMBIGUOUS, ESCALATE_POLICY_DECISION, DO_NOT_MERGE_CONFLICTING_IDENTIFIER, MAP_DIRECT, MAP_MANY_TO_ONE, MAP_SPLIT_REQUIRED, MAP_REQUIRES_ACCOUNTING_POLICY, EXCLUDE_OR_RETIRED_ACCOUNT.

## Observed in real data but described by no evidence extract (not taxonomy rows)

Listed so the next pass does not mistake them for omissions. Per the brief they are not defects here. A proposal to add one goes to `synthetic/PROPOSED_DEFECTS.md`.

- Different UEIs sharing one normalized street address and ZIP: 99 groups, 135 UEI pairs (`entities/profile.md`).
- Records with a long gap since last award activity (`first_seen` back to 2001). `evidence/estes_erp_pe_acquisitions.md` mentions obsolete *items*, not customers or vendors.

## Described by evidence but not applicable to this corpus

- Payment-term, credit-limit, pricing-rule and rebate conflicts (`evidence/estes_erp_pe_acquisitions.md`, `evidence/pmistack_erp_consolidation.md`): those fields are not in the real records and cannot be added without inventing values.
- Currency translation accounts (`evidence/houseblend_netsuite_100day.md`): every entity is a single-currency US business.
- Inconsistent units of measure and obsolete items (`evidence/estes_erp_pe_acquisitions.md`): item master is out of scope.
- Missing opening balances and prior trial balances (`evidence/consero_rollup_checklist.md`): transaction data is out of scope.
